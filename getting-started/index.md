# Getting Started

This guide explains how to invite Listune to your Discord server, configure permissions, and start playing music.

## Setup Workflow

Setting up Listune requires three steps:

1. **Invite the bot**: Authorize Listune through the Discord OAuth2 portal with appropriate channel permissions.
2. **Join a voice channel**: Connect to any server voice channel where you want music to play.
3. **Queue a track**: Use slash commands, prefix commands, or bot mentions to search for music or provide a direct link.
