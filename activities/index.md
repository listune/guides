## Supported Environments

Unlike traditional bots that only operate in guild voice channels, the Listune Activity operates across three distinct Discord environments:

### 1. Server Voice Channels
- **How to launch**: Join a voice channel in your Discord server, click the rocket **Activity** icon in voice controls, and select **Listune**.
- **Playback behavior**: The Activity synchronizes with the Main Bot in the voice channel. All participants inside the Activity share the same queue, synchronized playback, and interactive controls.

### 2. Direct Messages (DMs)
- **How to launch**: Open a private 1-on-1 Direct Message with a friend, click the rocket **Activity** icon, and launch Listune.
- **Playback behavior**: Does not require a bot voice channel. Audio streams directly through your Discord client via high-fidelity web audio inside the embedded application frame.
- **Use case**: Private personal listening or sharing music directly with a friend in a DM call.

### 3. Group Direct Messages (Group DMs)
- **How to launch**: Open any multi-user Group DM call and click the **Activity** icon.
- **Playback behavior**: Streams audio through the group activity frame, allowing group members to browse tracks, vote on songs, and enjoy music together in private group chats.

## Key Capabilities

- **Zero Tab Switching**: Access the complete Web Player interface without leaving Discord or opening an external browser window.
- **Full Library Access**: Browse your liked songs, custom playlists, listening history, and global radio stations directly within the activity modal.
- **Live Karaoke Lyrics**: Follow word-by-word synchronized lyrics while chatting with friends.
- **Adaptive Layout**: Automatically switches between a spacious focused layout on desktop displays and a compact layout on smaller windows and mobile devices.

## Requirements & Platform Support

- Supported across official Discord Desktop (Windows, macOS, Linux), Discord Web, and Discord Mobile applications that support Discord Activities.
- Requires completing the standard one-time Discord OAuth2 authorization prompt when launching the Activity for the first time.
