# Premium

Listune provides Premium subscription plans for individuals and Discord communities. Upgrading unlocks high-fidelity audio nodes, 24/7 voice channel connectivity, custom bot profiles, and expanded queue capacities.

## Free vs Premium Comparison

| Feature | Free Tier | Personal ($2/mo) | Guild Basic ($3/mo) | Guild Plus ($5/mo) |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **Price** | Free | $2.00 / month | $3.00 / month | $5.00 / month |
| **Activation Scope** | Public | 1 User Account | 1 Server Slot | 3 Server Slots |
| **Audio Quality** | Standard | High Fidelity | High Fidelity | High Fidelity |
| **Queue Track Limit** | 100 to 150 songs | 350 songs | 550 songs | Unlimited |
| **24/7 Voice Mode** | No | Yes (User sessions) | Yes (Whole server) | Yes (All 3 servers) |
| **Autoplay Mode** | Optional | Yes | Yes | Yes |
| **Volume Adjustment** | Default (80%) | Full (1% to 100%) | Full (1% to 100%) | Full (1% to 100%) |
| **Audio Filters Suite** | Standard | Full (26 filters) | Full (26 filters) | Full (26 filters) |
| **Bypass Vote Locks** | No | Yes | Yes (All members) | Yes (All members) |
| **Custom Bot Profile** | No | No | Yes (Name, Bio, Avatar, Banner) | Yes (All 3 servers) |
| **YouTube URL Handling** | Standard limits | Unlimited | Unlimited | Unlimited |
| **Discord Role & Support** | Community | Premium Role & Support | Premium Role & Support | Premium Role & Support |

## Subscription Plans

### Personal Plan ($2.00 / month)
- **Scope**: User account only.
- **Best for**: Individual listeners who use Listune across multiple Discord servers, Direct Messages, or the Web Player and want personal audio perks.
- **Patreon Checkout**: [Subscribe to Personal](https://www.patreon.com/checkout/listune?rid=22189390)

### Guild Basic Plan ($3.00 / month)
- **Scope**: 1 Server Slot.
- **Best for**: Server owners wanting 24/7 playback, custom bot branding, and unrestricted queue sizes for their entire community.
- **Patreon Checkout**: [Subscribe to Guild Basic](https://www.patreon.com/checkout/listune?rid=10026039)

### Guild Plus Plan ($5.00 / month)
- **Scope**: 3 Server Slots.
- **Best for**: Multi-server networks, gaming clans, and esports communities requiring premium music across multiple guilds with unlimited queue capacities.
- **Patreon Checkout**: [Subscribe to Guild Plus](https://www.patreon.com/checkout/listune?rid=10067355)
