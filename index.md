## Playback Environments

Listune provides three distinct ways to experience audio:

1. **Discord Voice Channels**: Control playback in server voice channels using slash commands, prefix commands, or direct bot mentions.
2. **Web Player & Dashboard**: Control queues, view live karaoke-style synchronized lyrics, and adjust volume in your browser at [listune.app/servers](https://listune.app/servers).
3. **Discord Embedded Activity**: Launch the embedded app directly inside Discord for interactive group listening in voice channels, direct messages, and group DMs.
